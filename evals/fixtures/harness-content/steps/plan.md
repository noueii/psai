# Plan (big tasks)

**In:** the spec. **Out:** a plan (templates/plan.md): steps, risks, test strategy.

- **At the `plan` node:** write or revise the plan, addressing every comment from the last review.
- **At the `plan_review` node:** show the plan to the human with a `review` block. Their answer selects `accepted` or `changes requested`. Never implement before `accepted`.
