# Do

**In:** the task, or the checker's findings when sent back. **Out:** the change and a short note of what you did.

Make the smallest change that does what the task asks. When sent back, address the findings, and only those.

**Edges:** `blocked` when the task can be read in more than one way, the readings give different results, and nothing in the repo settles it (for example "better" or "cleaner" with no criteria). Don't guess then: escalate. Otherwise the default.
