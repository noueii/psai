# Autonomy

- Agents run on their own. No skill is triggered by hand: no `disable-model-invocation`, no slash commands.
- Escalate only by the policy in [escalation.md](escalation.md). Otherwise decide, and log the decision and its reason in the PR description and the knowledge inbox.

Source: [decision 2026-11-21-autonomous](../decisions/2026-11-21-autonomous.md).
