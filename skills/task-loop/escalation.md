# Escalation

Escalate only when one of these holds:
1. A requirement is unclear AND the answer changes the outcome, and code, docs and knowledge can't settle it.
2. The action is irreversible or visible to others: merge, deploy, posting to people, deleting what isn't yours.
3. It goes against a stated rule or preference, or two rules conflict.
4. You're still stuck after two real attempts.
5. The scope grows well beyond what was asked.

Otherwise decide. Log the decision and its reason in the PR description and in the knowledge inbox (`capture-knowledge`).

## How to escalate
Ask one question at a time, through whatever channel the session gives you (the user, or a coordinator). Include:
- what you need decided, as a question with options and your recommendation;
- why the policy requires asking (which rule above);
- what you'll do meanwhile, if anything can continue.

Then stop the blocked part and carry on with work that doesn't depend on the answer.
